# The Daily Globe — Wednesday, November 25, 2026

The Wednesday, November 25, 2026 front page of *The Daily Globe*, a fictional
broadsheet. Day three of the Mediterranean crisis leads: Moscow delivers its
promised "answer" — Exercise Otvet, 38 warships and 14 Kalibr missiles off
Libya — as NATO invokes Article 4 and rushes the carrier Gerald R. Ford east.
Around it: oil tops $118 on day three of the Hormuz blockade; the island
nations win a March 9 hearing at The Hague for their $212 billion climate
suit; the first thousand of Jensen Huang's million robots clock in at
Nvidia's new Detroit robot city; and Tarik Skubal signs his $450 million
contract at Citi Field. Plus the weather, the markets, and Marmalade the
cat, home after nine years.

`entry.png` (1024×1024) is composed with Python + Pillow by
`make_front_page.py` at 2× supersample around an AI-generated news photo of
the missile launch.
